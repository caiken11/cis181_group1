Chicago Food Debates

Welcome to Chicago Food Debate, the ultimate arena for the city’s most delicious controversies. From the age-old battle of deep dish versus thin crust to the strictly forbidden act of putting ketchup on a hot dog, we are dedicated to dissecting the flavors that define the Windy City. Our mission is to fuel a community of passionate foodies, chefs, and casual diners through spirited rankings, neighborhood guides, and deep dives into Chicago’s culinary heritage. Whether you’re a lifelong local defending your favorite Italian beef or a curious visitor looking for the best tavern-style slice, we invite you to take a seat at the table and join the conversation.

Where Our Love of Food Meets Our Love of Chicago

Chicago Food Debate was born from two passions: the incredible food that defines our neighborhoods and the city that gives those flavors their identity. Every debate we highlight—pizza, sausage, beef, and beyond—is really a story about Chicago itself. The corner taverns, the family-run beef stands, the late-night slice spots, the immigrant traditions, and the fierce loyalty each neighborhood brings to its favorite foods all shape the way we eat.

Our love of food is inseparable from our love of Chicago. The dishes we celebrate aren’t just meals—they’re memories, histories, and symbols of community pride. By exploring these debates, we honor both the flavors that make Chicago iconic and the city that inspires them.
